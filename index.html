Who we are

Built on the yard, not the slide deck

Northbound started in 2009 with four trucks and a leased yard behind a steel plant. Sixteen years later we run nine hubs and a 46-country network — still owned by the same founding team, still answering our own phones.

  • Own fleet, own drivers. 340 tractors and 1,100 trailers, so peak season does not become a spot-market hostage situation.
  • Licensed in-house. Customs, dangerous goods and heavy-lift rigging certified under our own licences.
  • One control tower. Every lane, mode and document visible in a single view — no supplier finger-pointing.
  • Carbon reported. Per-shipment CO₂e on every invoice, audited to GHG Protocol scope 3.

How a shipment runs

Four steps, one thread of custody

No handovers to third parties, no gap in the paperwork trail. The same ops lead owns your file from booking to POD.

01

Scope & route

We profile the cargo — weight, centre of gravity, hazard class, dimensions — and return a costed route with a committed transit window.

02

Book & document

Capacity is secured, paperwork is drafted, and customs entries are pre-filed so nothing queues at the border on arrival.

03

Move & monitor

Geofenced tracking, exception alerts on delay or temperature drift, and a named control-tower operator for live calls.

04

Deliver & reconcile

Timed delivery with POD capture, demurrage reconciliation and a single consolidated invoice per shipment.
